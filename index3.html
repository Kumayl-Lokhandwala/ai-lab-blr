The PowerInfer Paradigm

Bypassing traditional hardware bottlenecks through dynamic workload routing.

Exploiting Neural Sparsity

Large Language Models exhibit a strict power-law distribution. During any given query, roughly 20% of the neural weights perform 80% of the mathematical heavy lifting.

Rather than purchasing ₹30 Lakh enterprise GPU clusters to load the entire model, this architecture surgically separates the workload, delivering high-end copilot speeds within standard workstation budgets.

Hot Neurons (High Frequency)

Pinned to dedicated NVIDIA RTX 4090 VRAM

~80%

Cold Neurons (Low Frequency)

Offloaded to massive Threadripper CPU RAM

~20%
