The PowerInfer Paradigm

Large Language Models exhibit a strict power-law distribution. During any given query, roughly 20% of the neural weights perform 80% of the mathematical heavy lifting.

Rather than purchasing ₹30 Lakh enterprise GPU clusters to load the entire model, this architecture surgically separates the workload.

Hot Neurons (High Frequency)
Pinned to NVIDIA RTX 4090 VRAM
~80%
Cold Neurons (Low Frequency)
Offloaded to Threadripper System RAM
~20%
